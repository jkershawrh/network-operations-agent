= 7. Deliver the NOC brief

Technical evidence becomes useful when an operator can make a bounded decision from it.

== Generate the brief inputs

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
jq '{
  incident:.alarm_id,
  hypothesis:.primary_hypothesis,
  current_evidence:[.current_observations_with_tool_provenance[]|{evidence_id,scope,state,provenance}],
  historical_sources:[.historical_context_with_source_revision[]|{source_id,source_revision}],
  unknowns:.unknowns_and_conflicts,
  next_test:.next_discriminating_test,
  proposed_action,
  action_requires_human_approval,
  action_executed
}' /tmp/upstream-result.json | tee "$HOME/network-agent-evidence/noc-decision-brief.json"
----

== Review as the approver

Your handoff must state:

. What is directly observed now.
. What comes from historical context.
. What the system infers from those facts.
. What remains unknown or conflicting.
. Which read-only test should happen next.
. That a qualified human—not the model—owns the operational decision.

[source,bash,role="execute"]
----
jq -e '.hypothesis.supporting_evidence_ids|length>0' "$HOME/network-agent-evidence/noc-decision-brief.json" && \
jq -e '.action_requires_human_approval and (.action_executed|not)' "$HOME/network-agent-evidence/noc-decision-brief.json"
----

You now have a reusable scenario pack, qualification evidence, and decision brief. Continue to xref:conclusion.adoc[the conclusion].
